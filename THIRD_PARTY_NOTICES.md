JUCE is fetched from its official repository or supplied through a local checkout; its license is in `third_party/JUCE/LICENSE.md` (or the fetched source). Binary release packages include that notice.

The embedded Inter SemiBold and JetBrains Mono Medium fonts are distributed under their SIL Open Font License notices in `Resources/fonts/Inter-LICENSE.txt` and `Resources/fonts/JetBrainsMono-LICENSE.txt`.

The VST3 SDK is supplied by JUCE. Its SDK and component license notices and agreement are included with binary release packages. AU builds use the system Audio Unit SDK. The `licenses/` folder holds copies of the notices for JUCE, the VST3 SDK, the AudioUnit SDK, WORLD and the libraries bundled with JUCE's audio and graphics modules.

These dependency notices do not select a license for this project's original source code.
